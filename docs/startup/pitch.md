# Aarogya Vault — Startup Pitch

## One-Liner

Aarogya Vault is an AI healthcare startup that gives Indians a secure, intelligent vault for their medical records — powered by Gemini AI and Google Cloud.

---

## The Pitch

Every year, Indian patients collect lab reports, prescriptions, and discharge summaries from dozens of providers. These documents end up scattered across WhatsApp chats, email inboxes, and paper folders — disconnected, unreadable, and often lost when they are needed most.

Aarogya Vault solves this with a simple loop: **upload, understand, access.**

Patients sign in with Google, upload a medical document, and our serverless pipeline does the rest. A Firebase Cloud Function triggers on every upload, sends the file to **Gemini 2.5 Flash** for medical OCR, and stores both the extracted text and a patient-readable AI summary in Firestore. The result is a personal health document vault that makes complex medical reports understandable — without replacing professional medical advice.

We are not building another hospital EMR. We are building the **missing personal layer** — the place where individuals own, organize, and understand their own health history.

**Amrit Kumar**, an Electronics & Computer Engineering graduate and full-stack developer, founded Aarogya Vault as a bootstrapped startup in India. He chose Google Cloud and Firebase as the production foundation from day one: Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Gemini AI, and Firebase Hosting. The MVP is live — a Flutter Android app, a deployed OCR pipeline, and a production website at **aarogyavault.live** with an active waitlist.

The product is technically complete. OCR processing is paused only because cloud credits are exhausted — not because of code or architecture limitations. We are applying to Google for Startups to resume AI processing, onboard beta users, and scale the infrastructure we have already built.

Our roadmap moves from today's document vault to AI health insights, family health management, and eventually healthcare ecosystem integrations across India's diagnostic and hospital networks. The vision is clear: **to become the personal health record layer for the next billion people.**

Aarogya Vault is early, bootstrapped, and founder-led — but it is real. The code is deployed. The website is live. The pipeline works. We need Google Cloud to turn a working MVP into a product that patients can actually use.
